Hero LiDAR point cloud (hero-pointcloud.json)

Sources: Neara Pole1, Pole3, Pole6, Pole8 .las files
https://github.com/Neara-Software/ground_segmentation_exercise

Outdoor iPad LiDAR captures. Elevated returns are clustered into object kinds:
  - pole (orange)
  - tree (pink)
  - structure (magenta)
  - ground (cyan)

Downsampled and normalised for the outrun grid projection.
